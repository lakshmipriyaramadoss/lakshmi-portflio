Price Elasticity Estimation Using Deep Neural Networks

Academic Project - Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan University

Developed a Deep Neural Network (DNN) model to estimate price elasticity and analyze demand sensitivity. Utilized historical sales data including price, quantity sold, discounts, and seasonal trends for model training.

Built predictive models to forecast demand at different price levels and support data-driven pricing decisions. Applied data preprocessing and feature selection techniques to improve model accuracy.

Demonstrated how AI-driven pricing strategies can optimize revenue and profitability for businesses.
